# Crew call-in plan

The selected operating day is the planning date; the plan covers the following
day's published JunkWare appointments. Suggestions are not confirmed availability.

Candidates must have positive recorded work hours in the planning date's current
pay period, through that planning date. Eligibility uses the shared Pay period
hours calculation, including applied payroll corrections and payroll-record
priority. Zero hours, unknown hours, revenue, job credits or bonuses alone do not
qualify. Work from a previous pay period never qualifies a candidate. Salaried
employees and people already assigned to target-day appointments remain excluded.

Recent production and driver history retain the 21-day performance window.
Current-week and projected hours use the shared corrected hours calculation.
The same eligibility gate applies to recommendations, alternates and the server's
validation of call-in decisions. A candidate shortage remains visible instead of
being filled with ineligible people.

Validation: `npm run verify:call-in-plan` and `npm run verify:payroll-review`.
