# Address verification completion

Every collected appointment address remains eligible for recovery and urgent oversight until verified, including completed, closed, canceled and unassigned records. Appointment outcome cannot clear an address failure. Square/SQ is a formatting equivalence; directions and house numbers must still match. Provider failures must remain visible and owned, with escalation. Verification cannot be fabricated to satisfy coverage. Evidence-backed source corrections use the durable reviewed-address store and preserve the original source field.
